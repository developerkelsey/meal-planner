  PRODUCE
    - 1 large head broccoli
    - 1 head green cabbage
    - 3 cucumbers
    - 2 pints cherry tomatoes
    - 3 red bell peppers

    - 2 heads romaine lettuce
    - 2 zucchini

    - 1 bunch asparagus
    - 4 lemons
    - 4 limes

    - 2 tubs Good Culture 2% cottage cheese (16 oz each)
    - 1/2 cup feta cheese

  PANTRY

    - 1 can diced tomatoes
    - 1 bottle teriyaki sauce
    - 1 small jar hoisin sauce
    - 1 loaf Dave's Killer sourdough bread
    - 8 oz Barilla whole wheat pasta
    - 2 lb bag jasmine rice
    - 1 lb bag brown rice
    - 1 small bag quinoa
    - 1 pack corn tortillas (8+)
    - Everything bagel seasoning
    - Dried oregano
    - Sesame seeds (optional)

  FROZEN
    - 1 lb frozen shrimp (31-40 count, peeled)
    - 1 bag frozen shelled edamame
